Refunds & Disputes

How to contact KampusX when a marketplace transaction has a problem.

Effective date: 23 October 2026

Important: Refunds depend on the transaction, the payment method used, and applicable law. Contact support as soon as you notice a problem.

1. When to contact support

Contact us if a payment appears incorrect, an order cannot be completed, an item materially differs from its listing, or you suspect fraud or unauthorised activity.

2. What to include

  • Your KampusX account email.
  • The listing or order identifier, if available.
  • The date and a clear description of what happened.
  • Relevant receipts, screenshots or messages. Do not send passwords or one-time security codes.

3. How to raise a concern

Email kampusx.mlx@gmail.com with the subject “KampusX transaction support”. You may also call +234 814 832 7363. We may ask for additional information reasonably needed to review the issue.

4. Review and outcomes

We will review the information provided and explain any next steps we can take. Some payment issues must be handled with the relevant payment provider. Any refund decision depends on the transaction facts, the applicable terms and policies, and legal requirements.

5. Preventing further loss

If you suspect unauthorised payment activity, contact the payment provider promptly as well as KampusX. Do not share passwords or verification codes with anyone claiming to be support.

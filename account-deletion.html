What happens when I delete my account?

  1. Your account is verified before deletion.
  2. Your authentication account and eligible profile data are removed by the deletion service.
  3. KampusX receives a limited operational notification containing your account email and any optional feedback, so the team can understand why people leave.
  4. Records that must be kept for legal, accounting, payment, fraud-prevention, or dispute reasons may be retained or anonymised as explained in the Privacy Policy.

If you have an unresolved order, payment, refund, or dispute, contact kampusx.mlx@gmail.com before deleting your account.

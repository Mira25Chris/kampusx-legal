Account Deletion

How to contact KampusX to request deletion of your account.

Effective date: 23 October 2026 · Draft — process must be implemented and tested

Developer action required: Publishing this page alone does not delete accounts. Before launch, implement and test an in-app deletion path and an external request process that the team can actually fulfil.

Request deletion by email

Send an email to kampusx.mlx@gmail.com with the subject KampusX account deletion request. Send the request from the email address associated with your account where possible.

In your message, state that you want your KampusX account deleted. Do not include your password or one-time verification codes. Support may need to verify that the request is authorised before acting on it.

What happens next?

The support team should confirm receipt, explain any verification needed and provide an update on the request. The actual response time must be established by KampusX before this page is finalised.

What information may be deleted?

The developer must determine which account and associated personal data can be deleted from active systems and how backups are handled. Some records may need to be retained for legal, fraud-prevention, accounting or dispute-resolution reasons. Any retained data, reasons and retention periods must be explained clearly.

Important availability note

This page describes the intended request channel. It does not mean in-app account deletion is already available. The developer must add and test that feature before submitting the app where Google Play requires it.

KampusX is a campus marketplace designed to help students discover, list, buy and sell items. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the app.

This is a draft. The developer must verify every section against the released app and its third-party services before publishing this policy or submitting it to Google Play.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, KampusX may process:

  • Account information: information such as your email address, account identifier, display name and profile details that you provide when registering or managing your account.
  • Campus information: the school or campus you select for your profile.
  • Marketplace content: listing titles, descriptions, prices, categories, item condition and images you upload.
  • Transactions: information needed to manage orders, purchase records, seller activity, payment status and related customer support.
  • Communications: information you provide when contacting support or using any messaging or communication feature available in the app.
  • Technical information: limited device, diagnostic or usage information if collected by the app, its hosting services or integrated software tools.

The exact information collected depends on the features enabled in the version you use. This list must be checked against the actual app and its software integrations.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and maintain accounts and profiles.
  • Display relevant campus marketplace listings.
  • Help buyers and sellers manage marketplace activity and orders.
  • Process and verify transactions through supported payment services.
  • Respond to support requests and investigate reported problems.
  • Protect accounts, prevent misuse and maintain app security.
  • Meet applicable legal and operational requirements.

3. Campus Listings and Visibility

Information you place in a marketplace listing may be visible to other KampusX users according to the app's listing and campus-access rules. Do not include passwords, sensitive personal information or other details you do not want other users to see in public listing content.

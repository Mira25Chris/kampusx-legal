Privacy Policy

How KampusX handles information when you use our campus marketplace.

Effective date: 23 October 2026 · Last reviewed: 23 October 2026

Publication checklist: This policy is a working draft and must be checked against the released app, backend configuration and every SDK or service integration before it is submitted to Google Play. Do not publish it as final until those checks are complete.

KampusX is a campus marketplace designed to help students discover, list, buy and sell items. This policy explains the categories of information that may be processed when you use the app.

1. Information that may be processed

  • Account details: email address, account identifier, display name and profile information you provide.
  • Campus details: the school or campus selected for your profile.
  • Listing content: item titles, descriptions, prices, categories, condition and images you upload.
  • Order and transaction information: order records, payment status, seller activity and information needed to support a transaction.
  • Support communications: information you include when contacting KampusX.
  • Technical and diagnostic information: information processed by the app or its integrated services, where applicable. The exact fields must be verified before this policy is finalised.

2. How information may be used

Information may be used to operate accounts, show relevant listings, support marketplace activity, manage transactions, answer support requests, protect the service against misuse, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Listing visibility

Information included in a listing may be visible to other users according to KampusX's campus and access rules. Do not include passwords or sensitive personal information in listing content.

4. Service providers and sharing

KampusX uses Supabase for backend functionality. Flutterwave may be used for supported marketplace payment transactions. These providers may process information needed to provide their services, subject to their own terms and privacy practices. The developer must confirm the exact data each provider receives, as well as all other SDKs, integrations and recipients included in the released app.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law or where reasonably necessary to investigate fraud, protect users or maintain service security.

5. Payments

Payment processing may be handled by a third-party payment provider. The precise payment details and transaction records stored by KampusX or its providers must be verified. Do not assume that KampusX stores full payment-card details.

6. Storage, security and retention

Information is handled through the app's backend and relevant service providers. Appropriate safeguards should be used, but no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Actual access controls, backup arrangements and retention periods must be verified and documented by the developer before this policy is treated as final.

7. Your choices and account deletion

Users need a clear way to request account deletion and understand how associated personal data is handled. Use the Account Deletion page for the current instructions. The developer must ensure that the in-app deletion path and external request method both work before launch. Some records may be retained when required by law; any exceptions should be explained clearly.

8. Children's privacy and age eligibility

KampusX must follow applicable age-related laws and platform requirements. The app's intended audience and eligibility rules should be accurately stated in its store listing and product configuration.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or account-deletion requests, email kampusx.mlx@gmail.com or call +234 814 832 7363.
